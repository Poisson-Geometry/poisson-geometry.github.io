 Poisson 2018 - International Conference on Poisson Geometry
 July 16 - 20, 2018 , The Fields Institute 
 Description
 Poisson 2018 is the 11th in a series of international conferences on Poisson geometry. It will be held at the University of Toronto St. George Campus, Earth Sciences Centre (5 Bancroft Avenue) Room 1050 
 We will be hosting a poster session on Tuesday during the reception, which will be held at the Fields Institute. We are expecting 10-15 posters at this point, if you have not arleady arranged printing you may find information here useful. Please drop posters off to the Program Coordinators office at Fields any time during opening hours, or simply bring the poster directly with you to the reception.  
 For details on previous conferences, visit the  Poisson Geometry Website .
 Andre Lichnerowicz Prize in Poisson geometry 
 The Andre Lichnerowicz Prize in Poisson geometry was established in 2008.
 It is awarded for notable contributions to Poisson geometry, every two years at the "International Conference on Poisson Geometry in Mathematics and Physics", to researchers who completed their doctorates at most eight years before the year of the Conference.
 The prize is named in memory of Andre Lichnerowicz (1915-1998) whose work was fundamental in establishing Poisson geometry as a branch of mathematics. It is awarded by a jury composed of the members of the scientific/advisory committee of the conference.
 The prize for the year 2018 was awarded to Brent Pym and Chelsea Walton .

 Who we are
 The Fields Institute is a centre for mathematical research activity - a place where mathematicians from Canada and abroad, from academia, business, industry and financial institutions, can come together to carry out research and formulate problems of mutual interest. Our mission is to provide a supportive and stimulating environment for mathematics innovation and education. Learn more about us. 
 The Fields Institute promotes mathematical activity in Canada and helps to expand the application of mathematics in modern society.
 Everyone is welcome to register and participate in events at the Fields Institute.
