About the prize

Established in 2008, the André Lichnerowicz Prize recognizes outstanding contributions to Poisson geometry by early-career researchers. Awarded biennially in connection with the International Conference on Poisson Geometry, it commemorates André Lichnerowicz (1915–1998), whose work helped establish the subject.

The prize is selected by a jury associated with the conference's scientific committee, with eligibility based on early-career research experience. Read the 2026 prize criteria.
