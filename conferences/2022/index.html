Programme & themes

The 12th international Poisson conference took place on 25–29 July 2022. Its booklet describes a joint CRM–ICMAT intensive research programme, with an advanced school in Barcelona preceding the Madrid conference. The schedule distinguishes the opening day at ICMAT from the following days at the CSIC Central Building. The programme includes Hamiltonian spaces, quantization, symplectic foliations, Lie groupoids and algebroids.
