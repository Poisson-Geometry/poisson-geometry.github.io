Programme & themes

The Naples conference ran from 8 to 12 July 2024 at the Accademia Pontaniana. The booklet lists 20 scheduled plenary talks, alongside a poster programme. Themes include shifted and derived geometry, groupoids, quantization, moduli spaces, dynamics and mathematical physics. The timetable places the poster buffet on Wednesday 10 July.

Inspect source · PDF p. 2

Yunhe Sheng’s slides connect post-groups and post-groupoids with Rota–Baxter operators, braces, Butcher groups and Yang–Baxter equations. The title page records 10 July 2024.

Inspect source · PDF p. 1

Henrique Bursztyn’s handwritten slides present symplectic groupoids through 2-shifted Lagrangian structures and credit joint work with D. Álvarez and M. Cueca.
