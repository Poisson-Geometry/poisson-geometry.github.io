Programme & themes

The 11th international edition met in Toronto on 16–20 July 2018. The programme includes plenary talks on symplectic foliations, deformation quantization, Dirac geometry and Poisson–Lie theory. The official record lists a reception and poster session at the Fields Institute and identifies Brent Pym and Chelsea Walton as the 2018 Lichnerowicz Prize recipients.
