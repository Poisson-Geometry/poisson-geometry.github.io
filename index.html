The mathematics

What is Poisson geometry?

Poisson geometry is the study of smooth manifolds equipped with a Poisson structure—a Lie bracket on smooth functions satisfying the Leibniz rule. It extends symplectic geometry and the geometric formulation of classical mechanics; through deformation quantization, the bracket also appears as the first-order, semiclassical limit of a quantum commutator.

{f, gh} = {f, g}h + g{f, h}

The Poisson bracket is named after the nineteenth-century French mathematician Siméon Denis Poisson. The subject brings together differential geometry, Lie theory, and mathematical physics.
